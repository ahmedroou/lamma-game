Solar System Facts Planets Moons Solar System Facts Our solar system includes the Sun, eight planets, five officially named dwarf planets, hundreds of moons, and thousands of asteroids and comets. Our solar system is located in the Milky Way, a barred spiral galaxy with two major arms, and two minor arms. Our Sun is in a small, partial arm of the Milky Way called the Orion Arm, or Orion Spur, between the Sagittarius and Perseus arms. Our solar system orbits the center of the galaxy at about 515,000 mph (828,000 kph). It takes about 230 million years to complete one orbit around the galactic center. NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute Farthest ever, moon or mars Out-of-This-World Record: Perseverance Goes the Distance Since it landed on the Red Planet in 2021 to search for signs of ancient life, NASA’s Perseverance Mars rover has driven farther, faster than any vehicle ever has on the surface of another world. On Oct. 11, its odometer reached 28.10 miles (45.22 kilometers), rolling past the previous record of 28.06 miles (45.16 kilometers) by NASA’s Opportunity rover (2004-2018). Doubly remarkable, Perseverance has covered the ground in about 5½ years, compared to almost 15 for its predecessor.
