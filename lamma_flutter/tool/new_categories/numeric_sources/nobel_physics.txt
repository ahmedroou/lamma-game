Search All Nobel Prizes in Physics The Nobel Prize in Physics has been awarded 120 times to 231 Nobel Prize laureates between 1901 and 2026. John Bardeen is the only laureate who has been awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics twice, in 1956 and 1972. This means that a total of 230 individuals have received the Nobel Prize in Physics.
